# Independent source-first context review: Algebra tail

Reviewer: authoritative_overlays. Source revision: a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14.

Read algebra.tex 9514–10036 in physical order, with proof environments removed before displaying text, before inspecting the new reconstructions. The adjacent support statements were also read for notation. This covers both complete source sections: Going up and going down, and Separable extensions. No proof body was read or screened.

- A specialization of a prime corresponds to containment in the increasing direction. Going up lifts increasing chains from a prescribed lower prime; going down lifts them from a prescribed upper prime (9531–9548). Topological lifts must start at the specified source point, not simply assert existence of some point over the target.
- The cases listed at 9551–9563 are examples, not standing assumptions on later ring maps. In particular neither integrality nor flatness is inherited by the going-up/closed-map equivalence.
- The constructible-set theorem has two independently guarded implications: specialization stability implies closedness; generalization stability implies openness.
- The openness proposition has both the flat finite-presentation case and the more general finite-presentation plus going-down assertion. The latter is not a conjunction with flatness.
- The tensor-algebra image statement is over a field, with a specified subalgebra and specified element of its tensor product. The localization element upstairs is the image of that same element.
- For unique-prime localization (9796–9812), uniqueness over the chosen prime is a common antecedent. The second alternative additionally requires at most one prime over every prime of the base, not merely uniqueness over the selected one. The claimed equality compares the canonical localizations S_p=(R minus p)^(-1)S and S_q.
- In the flat-module version (9851–9856), finite means finitely generated as an S-module, whereas flatness is over R. Generalizations lift through the support equipped with the induced topology; no ambient Noetherian condition is present.
- Separable generation and separability are explicitly defined for possibly transcendental extensions at 9914–9925. Separably generated means algebraic separability after choosing a transcendence basis. Separable means every finitely generated intermediate extension is separably generated. An algebraic-only definition would change the source meaning.
- The r+1 generators in 9944–9955 are a common existential tuple: its first r elements form the transcendence basis, the full tuple generates the field, and the final element is separable over the same rational function field.
- The purely inseparable enlargement in 9961–9973 is one commutative field-embedding diagram; both finite purely inseparable extensions must coexist so that K-prime over k-prime is separably generated. No characteristic restriction is introduced.
