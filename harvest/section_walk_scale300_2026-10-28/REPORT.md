## Context handling

1. **Physical scoping:** All 364 entered source environments have corresponding exits. All 300 active candidate statements have their exact source location and closing environment line. No premise from one lemma automatically carries into the next. Eleven source formal statements not in the active pool were traversed without silently changing candidate counts.
2. **One chapter boundary:** Chapter-local standing assumptions are reset when moving from the last Coherent section to the Divisors Introduction. Previously encountered mathematical definitions remain addressable *as terminology*, never auto-added as hypotheses.
3. **35 named definitions:** Source-labelled definitions include associated and weakly associated points, Fitting-related topics, effective Cartier divisors, conormal and normal cones, regular immersions, meromorphic sections, Weil divisors, blowing up, and strict transforms. Their actual source positions are recorded.
4. **Two additional definitions from prose:** `Coh(X, I)` is defined in the Coherent formal modules section through an inverse-system compatibility condition. A norm of degree `d` is defined in the Divisors Norms section by a multiplicative sheaf map with its associated power/vanishing properties. These term meanings are source-anchored; they do not create unconditional chapter hypotheses.
5. **Eight prose leads adjudicated:** The Coherent chapter's “throughout this section we will use” remarks invoke known mathematical facts, not an added ambient Noetherian hypothesis for each lemma. Similarly, Divisors source paragraphs about associated primes, conormal algebra, norms, immersions and meromorphic sections are either definitions/caveats or expository notation, not licenses to import earlier theorem premises wholesale.
6. **Safe restart state:** The 54 [section checkpoints](SECTION_CHECKPOINTS.jsonl) record exact source ends and active candidate IDs, cumulative progress, available source-defined terms and an empty local-hypothesis stack. The last checkpoint is after `divisors.tex:9492`. At the actual chapter boundary, the reset is marked explicitly.

## Logical structure and quality controls

The source-first reconstruction explicitly distinguishes:

- **23** equivalence-form statements: every enumerated alternative remains a condition in the equivalence, never an automatically inherited theorem hypothesis.
- **22** lists of explicit premises: items following “Assume” are treated as hypotheses with the actual consequent kept separate.
- **48** multipart assertions: individual “If”/“Suppose” guards are retained rather than applied globally.
- **8** nested or multiple enumerations: source statements preserved in full and held for further quantifier review.
- Shared existential witnesses and alternative conditional hypothesis lists are kept grouped; ambiguous examples remain held rather than being flattened into unrelated claims.

The earlier Pass 2 suggestion fields are retained for audit, but the source text governs the draft. In particular, a discovered error during this run—enumerated assumptions being treated as conclusions—was corrected and backed by dedicated Python regression tests.

### Why 58 remain held

The **58 holds are individual candidate-level statuses**, and issues can overlap:

- **43** involve source labels or cross-references whose role in the mathematical statement still needs explicit linking/expansion; some will turn out to be editorial and can be resolved in a bounded pass.
- **8** have nested/multiple enumerations requiring a more careful quantifier and dependency check.
- Several contain “if one of the following,” one shared existential witness, or another conditional structure requiring manual source-equivalence confirmation.
- A few import a named functor, notation or assumptions defined outside the statement (notably the completion functor and a particular strict-transform context).

The held statements are **not lost or rejected**. The complete original source statement, candidate identity, source URL, tentative structured logic and explicit reason for holding are saved in the matching `sNN_reviews.jsonl` file. No recursive mathematical proof research was undertaken to clear them.

## Current throughput interpretation

The previous complete-section pilot batches handled **30 drafts in 2 sections**, **74 in 5 sections**, and **150 in 19 sections**. This experiment reached **300 drafts in 54 consecutive sections**. The batch completed successfully; no arbitrary 300-candidate cut or lost section occurred.

The draft-ready rate is **242/300 (80.7%)**, compared to **134/150 (89.3%)** in the previous Algebra run. This is not necessarily a speed decline: the present chapters contain more named source dependencies and intricate nested statements. No controlled elapsed-time comparison has been made, and the largest sustainable **final mathematical validation** batch size remains unknown.

**Practical observation:** 300 is feasible for source-context reconstruction with conservative holds and validation. Before scaling much further, a useful next experiment would be to improve and assess semantic quality of the 242 draft-ready statements and resolve some of the 58 holds—not to build a large new metadata subsystem or begin Pass 3 prematurely.
